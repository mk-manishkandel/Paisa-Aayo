## 1. What the app does

Paisa Aayo reads payment notifications that other apps (such as eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay and banks)
show on your phone and announces the amount aloud. It is a convenience tool. It is **not** a bank,
wallet or payment service: it does not send, receive, hold or verify money, and it is not
affiliated with or endorsed by any payment provider or bank.

## 2. Always confirm payments yourself

Announcements depend on notifications from other apps, on your phone's settings and on Android.
A payment may be missed, delayed or announced incorrectly. For example, a notification may be late
or hidden by the system, or worded in a way the app doesn't recognise, and a fake notification
from another app or SMS could be announced too. **Before handing over goods or money, confirm
important payments in your payment app or bank statement.** Do not rely on the announcement alone.
